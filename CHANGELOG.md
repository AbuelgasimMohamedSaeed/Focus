# Changelog

## Web 1.5.0 (2026-11-13): Leaderboard
- Progress has a switch at the top: My progress or Leaderboard.
- One board for everyone who uses Focus. Join by typing your name; the same name on several devices adds up as one person.
- Today or this week (from Monday), with each person's days in a row. Only names and daily focus minutes are shared.

## Android app (2026-11-13): your own prayer times
- The five prayer times are a list. Change any time to match your mosque; Focus keeps that difference (say +3 min) every day. The ↺ button goes back to the calculated time.

## Android app (2026-11-13): prayer reminder
- Prayer time is now a reminder, not a block: at each adhan, whatever app you're in stops with "Time for Asr" until you tap "I understand", then it carries on. One tap per prayer.
- It works with Track only too, and covers every app, not only watched ones.
- Prayer times follow your location (rough location, refreshed at most every 6 hours when you open Focus). Without location, Focus uses the city that matches your phone's time zone. The method (JAKIM, Egyptian, Umm al-Qura, Gulf, Turkey, Karachi, MWL, ISNA) is the one used in the nearest listed city.

## Android app (2026-11-13): App Time, stage 4
- Open hours per app: tap a watched app, then Add hours (for example Snapchat only 7 to 8:30 AM and 6 to 9 PM). Outside them the app shows a block screen saying when it opens.
- Bedtime: watched apps stay closed overnight (11 PM to 6 AM by default).
- Prayer times: watched apps close at each adhan for 10 to 30 minutes. Times are calculated on the phone for Kuala Lumpur (JAKIM), Khartoum, Makkah (Umm al-Qura) or Cairo; today's times show on the page.
- Emergency unlocks also work on these block screens (15 minutes).
- With a delay set, turning off or changing bedtime, prayer times or an app's hours waits for the delay too.

## Android app (2026-11-13): PIN removed
- The PIN to pause limits is gone, from the App Time page and the block screen.

## Android app 1.8 (2026-11-13): App Time, stage 3
- Unlock 5 more minutes from the block screen, after waiting 30 seconds or typing a sentence (or not at all).
- Emergency unlocks: 15 minutes with no questions, 1, 3 or 5 per week.
- Break before reopening: a limited app stays closed for 5, 10 or 15 minutes after you leave it.
- Delay before loosening: lowering or removing limits, turning on Track only or weakening any rule takes effect only after 10 minutes to an hour. Tightening is instant.

## 1.4.0 (2026-11-13)
- Theme: Dark or Light (Progress › Settings).
- Background photos: pick a topic (Nature, Animals, Birds, Flowers, Sea, Night sky, Forest), get one photo or download 8. Photos come from Wikimedia Commons' featured pictures (Picsum/Unsplash as a backup), with the photographer credited; downloaded photos work offline.

## Android app 1.7 (2026-11-13): App Time, stage 2
- Daily limit per watched app (tap the app on the App Time page): 10 minutes to 2 hours.
- When an app's time is up, a full block screen covers it until tomorrow; Back and "Close it" go to the home screen.
- Track only switch: keep the numbers, block nothing.
- Uses the same Accessibility permission as the top line. It only sees which app is open, and sets one timer per opened app, so it costs almost no battery.

## 1.3.0 (2026-11-13)
- Labels: one small chip at the top left of Sit. Tap it to choose a label, add one with its own color, or delete one. Someone new starts with Study, Work and Reading.
- Sit shows only the time and Start; bell sound, interval bell and count up/down moved to the settings button next to Start.

## Android app 1.6 (2026-11-13)
- Mini button opens Focus in the phone's own floating window (Xiaomi's, Samsung's…) when the phone allows it; otherwise Focus's own mini window.
- Focus's own mini window keeps its shape when you resize it, and everything inside grows and shrinks with it. Pull the line at the bottom down (or up) for full screen; the expand button is gone.
- The top line is thicker, so you can see it at a glance.
- Dhikr: History moved to a button at the top; no Undo; no instructions while counting — just the counter.

## Android app 1.5 (2026-11-13)
- Silence button during a sit (top left, and in the mini window): mutes the bells and the ticking; vibration stays.
- The top line now disappears whenever Focus is open.
- The mini window lays itself out again after you resize it.

## Android app 1.4 (2026-11-13)
- Mini window looks like Xiaomi's floating windows: one surface in the page's color (white at a Dhikr goal), three dots on top to move it, a curved corner to resize it.
- A line at the bottom of the mini window: tap it or swipe it up to open the full app.
- Opening Focus in Xiaomi's own floating window (or split screen) switches it to the small timer / counter view by itself.

## Android app 1.3 (2026-11-13)
- Line at the top of the screen now draws over the status bar, through Focus's Accessibility service (which reads nothing on screen). It redraws only when it would move a pixel, and never with the screen off.
- Mini window opens small (Sit timer or Dhikr) and can be resized from its bottom-right corner; it remembers the size.
- Dhikr vibration goes through Android itself: a strong tap on each of the last 10, a double buzz at the goal.
- New Tick sound: a soft tick-tock every second while the sit runs (with the Ring bell at the start and end).
- Dhikr history moved behind a History button, so the Dhikr screen is only the counter.
- The Urge screen is removed completely, along with its "urges beaten" count on Progress.

## Android app 1.2 (2026-11-13): App Time, stage 1
- New App Time page (Android): minutes and number of opens per app today, read from Android's own usage records.
- Pick which apps to watch; add or remove any app, with search.
- Settings › App Time page switch hides the page completely.
- Needs "Usage access", which the page walks you through. Everything stays on the device.
