    Block the apps for real

    iPhone

    1. Settings → Screen Time → App Limits → Add Limit.
    2. Tick Social, set 1 minute a day, turn on Block at End of Limit.
    3. Let a friend set your Screen Time passcode so you can't skip it.

    Android

    1. Settings → Digital Wellbeing & parental controls → Dashboard.
    2. Tap each social app → App timer → 1 minute.
    3. Or turn on Focus mode and add the social apps to it.

    The strongest option is deleting the apps from your phone and only checking them on your laptop.
